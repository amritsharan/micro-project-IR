What is BM25?

BM25 (Best Match 25) is a probabilistic ranking function widely used in modern search engines and information retrieval systems. It combines term frequency, inverse document frequency, and document length normalization to produce highly relevant search results.

BM25 is considered superior to traditional TF-IDF because it accounts for document length saturation, preventing long documents from dominating results.

Probabilistic: Based on probability theory and information retrieval models
Length-aware: Normalizes scores based on document length
Tunable: Configurable parameters for different use cases

The Formula

score(D,Q) = Σ IDF(q)
· (f(q,D) · (k₁ + 1))
────────────────────
f(q,D) + k₁(1 - b
+ b · |D| / avgdl)

Where each term in the query contributes to the total relevance score based on its frequency in the document and across the corpus.

Parameters

  • IDF(q) Inverse Document Frequency of query term — measures rarity across corpus
  • f(q,D) Term frequency of query term in document D
  • |D| Length of document D (in words/tokens)
  • avgdl Average document length in the entire corpus
  • k₁ Controls term frequency saturation (default: 1.5)
  • b Controls length normalization (0.0 = none, 1.0 = full)

Parameter Tuning

k₁ (1.5 default): Higher values increase term frequency impact. Range: 0.5-3.0

b (0.75 default): Controls length normalization strength. 0 = off, 1 = full

For short texts like tweets: Lower k₁ (0.9) and higher b (0.8)

For long documents: Higher k₁ (2.0) and lower b (0.3)
